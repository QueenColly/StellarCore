UI/UX Design (Light Mode)

Footer Component: Designed a responsive footer with consistent branding, validated navigation links, read-only context, and desktop/mobile layouts.
Open Dashboard: Designed a closing CTA that navigates to the read-only /dashboard route, including hover and keyboard-focus states.
The Product Contract (Four Promises): Designed a four-promise section communicating read-only visibility, separated evidence layers, transparent limitations, and explainability.
Reputation Evidence: Designed a definition panel explaining persisted reputation evaluations, absent trusted outcomes, unpublished scores, and insufficient evidence without fabricating reputation scores.
