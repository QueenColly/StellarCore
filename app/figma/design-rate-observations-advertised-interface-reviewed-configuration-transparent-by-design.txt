UI/UX Design (Light Mode)

Rate Observations: Present stored SEP-38 indicative rates alongside their observation timestamps and source counts. Include clear handling for unavailable medians and conditional data freshness without implying live rates.
Advertised Interface: Show interfaces obtained from the last successful SEP-1 synchronization, with clear messaging that advertised capabilities do not confirm current operational availability.
Reviewed Configuration: Organize reviewed anchors, corridors, and rate sources while keeping configured values visually separate from persisted observations.
Transparent By Design: Communicate evidence origins, uncertainty, and product limitations so users can distinguish verified information from assumptions or unavailable evidence.
