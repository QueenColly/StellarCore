UI/UX Design (Light Mode)

1. Corridors Section:
Present reviewed corridors through their source, destination, associated anchors, asset details, and country labels. Make it explicit that reviewed corridor membership does not establish a verified or executable transfer route.

2. Reputation Section
Show persisted reputation evaluations with clear explanations of evidence sufficiency. Only present a score when the available outcome evidence supports one; avoid assigning credibility to missing or insufficient evidence.

3. Horizontal Inspection Overview 
Create a desktop-oriented composition that separates the four inspection categories:

- Anchor metadata
- Reviewed corridors
- Rate observations
- Reputation evidence

4. Vertical Inspection Overview
Provide a mobile-friendly vertical arrangement of the same four categories, preserving their meaning, distinction, and intended reading sequence without introducing additional functionality
